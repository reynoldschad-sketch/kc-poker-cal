What this is

Live tournament poker within a four-hour drive of Kansas City. Every room in the metro including dailies; outside it, only $250-and-up buy-ins and named tour stops. No bar poker, no free leagues.

Where it comes from

Casino and tour schedules, read directly from the operators wherever possible. Every event links to the page it came from and carries the date it was last checked. Open any event to see both.

How sure it is

Each event is tagged certain, likely, or guessing. Anything below certain is drawn with a dotted ring on the grid. Schedules change without notice. Call the room before you drive.

Distances

Measured from a home address you set yourself, never from your location. The header shows whether times are routed or estimated. Estimated times are good to roughly ten percent, not to the minute.
